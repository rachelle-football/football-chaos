Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Football Chaos

🎯 Getting Started

What age is Football Chaos designed for? +

Football Chaos is designed for kids ages 6-14. The program scales to different skill levels—beginners find the challenges achievable and confidence-building, while more advanced players find progression and depth in the 4-week plan.

Do they need to be on a soccer team to use Football Chaos? +

No! Football Chaos works great whether your child is on a competitive team, a casual league, or just plays in the backyard. The program is designed to complement any level of involvement.

Is this a digital or physical workbook? +

Football Chaos comes as a digital PDF workbook that you can print at home or view on a tablet. Print it to follow along during training sessions, or use it digitally—whatever works best for your family!

What's the parent guide for? +

The 4-page parent guide gives you everything you need to support your child's training journey. It includes week-by-week expectations, how to motivate your child, what to watch for in their progress, and solutions to common challenges parents face.

🏋️ Training Details

How much time does Football Chaos take each week? +

About 2-3 hours per week total. This breaks down to roughly 15-20 minutes on workbook activities (understanding concepts, filling out the exercises) and 20-30 minutes of actual drills and practice. Most families find this easily fits into a regular training schedule.

Does my child need a soccer ball to do Football Chaos? +

Yes, they'll need a soccer ball (any size works), a space to practice (backyard, field, park), and ideally a wall for some drills. Most activities adapt to different space sizes—you don't need a full field.

Can they do it by themselves or do they need a coach? +

Most activities kids can do independently, but having a parent present helps with motivation and safety. The parent guide prepares you to support them. The YouTube coaching videos embedded in each drill provide live guidance.

What if they skip a week? Do they start over? +

No! The program is flexible. If they miss a week, just pick up where they left off. Football skills build cumulatively, so any missed week can be made up. Some families spread 4 weeks over 6 weeks—it's about consistency, not speed.
