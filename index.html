Frequently Asked Questions

What age is Football Chaos for?

Ages 6-14. The activities scale to different skill levels.

Do they need a soccer ball?

Yes, but any size ball works. Instructions adapt to backyard or field.

How much time per week?

About 2 hours/week: 15-20 min workbook activities, 20-30 min drills.

Do they need to be on a team?

No! Works great for kids at any skill level, team or non-team players.
